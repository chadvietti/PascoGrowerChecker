How the numbers are made. Harvests aren't weighed whole. Est. lbs = harvested gutters × that lot's same-day 2-gutter sample lbs/gutter (or the lot's average if no same-day sample), with blank 0-lbs samples left out. Weekly figures weight each entry by its gutters.

Yield vs baseline compares each harvest week with the seasonal lbs/gutter on the 2027 Projection tab (Winter Dec–Feb, Spring Mar–May, Summer Jun–Aug, Fall Sep–Nov), including any adjustment %. Flags: ≥15% below = low, 5–15% below = below, ≥10% above = above. A week is shown once harvests exist after its Friday.

Compartment gaps are flagged when the weekly C1−C2 gap exceeds the measure's threshold (temp 1.0 °C, daily min 1.5 °C, RH 5%, HD 0.8 g/m³, DLI 1.0 mol, misting 1.5 h) and at least 6 of 7 days point the same way.
